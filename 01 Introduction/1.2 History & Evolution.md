## The Origin
* **Creator:** Bjarne Stroustrup
* **Location:** AT&T Bell Labs
* **Timeline:** Started in *1979* as "C with Classes" and officially renamed to *C++* in *1983*.
* **The Goal:** Stroustrup wanted to combine the incredible speed and hardware efficiency of *C* with the high-level organizational features of *Simula* (the first object-oriented language).

## Why the name "C++"?
The name is a clever programming pun. The `++` symbol is the *increment operator* in C. Therefore, "C++" literally translates to *"one  step ahead of C*" or *"incremented C"*.

## Major Modern Standard Milestones
| Standard  | Release Year | Significance                                                                                           |
| --------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **C++98** | 1998         | The first official, standardized ISO version.                                                          |
| **C++11** | 2011         | **"Modern C++"**. Revolutionized the language with auto types, smart pointers, and lambda expressions. |
| **C++17** | 2017         | Added structured bindings, filesystem support, and standard library optimizations.                     |
| **C++20** | 2020         | Introduces Modules, Concepts, Ranges, and Coroutines (huge paradigm shift).                            |
| **C++23** | 2023         | Refinements, standard library modularization (`import std;`).                                          |
